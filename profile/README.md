# Breachday — Cybersecurity Tabletop Exercise Platform

**Spot your gaps. Secure your future.**

Breachday is a browser-based platform for running Business Continuity (BC) and Incident Response (IR) tabletop exercises. Facilitators control the session in real time; participants join from any device using a 4-character room code — no accounts, no installs, no setup friction.

Every exercise ends with an audit-ready report mapped to the controls your compliance team actually needs.

---

## What We Build

### The Live Exercise Platform

A synchronized inject timeline, role-based seating, decision votes, freeform responses, and situation updates — all captured against a per-inject record that becomes your after-action report. The exercise hour itself is the audit artifact.

**Participants join in seconds:**

1. Go to `app.breachday.io/join`
2. Enter the 4-character room code
3. Claim a role seat (Incident Commander, Legal, Comms, or any role your org defines)
4. Start responding

Works on phones, tablets, and laptops. No onboarding friction for executives, legal counsel, or non-technical stakeholders.

## Compliance Coverage

Breachday sessions generate structured evidence mapped to the controls auditors check:

- **SOC 2** — Incident response and availability controls (CC7.x)
- **PCI DSS 4.0** — Requirement 12.10.1 incident response plan testing
- **SEC Item 1.05** — Cyber incident response readiness for the 4-day disclosure rule
- **ISO 22301** — Business Continuity Management exercise and review evidence
- **CISA CTEP** — Cybersecurity Tabletop Exercise Package-aligned scenario templates

---

## Scenario Methodology

Hypothetical scenarios are easy to dismiss. Our library is built from:

- **Actual public breaches** — wire fraud incidents, ransomware events, identity provider lockouts, insider data theft, and more
- **CISA CTEP templates** — exercise packages from the U.S. Cybersecurity & Infrastructure Security Agency, adapted for sector-specific resilience

Your team rehearses the exact patterns attackers and operational failures keep using, with decision points the U.S. government recommends.

## Our Principles

1. **Realistic pressure** — Timed injects and incomplete information simulate the fog of war. Linear slide decks do not.
2. **Low friction participation** — Executives and external counsel shouldn't need a tutorial. A room code and a simple UI make joining effortless.
3. **Actionable outcomes** — If a tabletop doesn't result in concrete improvements to runbooks or architecture, it was wasted time. Our lessons learned tracker ensures follow-through.
4. **Compliance-ready by default** — Every exercise produces an audit-grade artifact mapped to SOC 2, PCI DSS, SEC, and ISO controls — without a separate writing project.
